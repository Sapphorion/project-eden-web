PROJECT EDEN

You have an island, a budget and a genetics lab. Lay roads, fence paddocks, run power and raise your park rating to unlock bigger attractions, from aviaries to a Mosasaurus lagoon.

Leave it be and the park ticks along, earning while you watch. Every new build adds strain, and once strain passes 22% the park can fail at any moment, day or night. When it does, you are on the ground with whatever you can find, and the volcano is waking up.
