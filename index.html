PROJECT EDEN

You have an island, a budget and a genetics lab. Lay roads, fence paddocks, run power and raise your park rating to unlock bigger attractions, from aviaries to a Mosasaurus lagoon.

The park runs in real time: one park day is one real day, with real day and night, and it keeps earning while you are away. Keep an eye on system strain in the control room as you grow. Monte Ceniza smokes quietly in the background.

On a phone: drag with one finger to pan or build, pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to rotate, tap a building for its info. Turn your phone sideways for the best view.
