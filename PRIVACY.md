# Project Eden privacy notice

**Effective date:** 3 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information Project Eden ("the Game") processes, why, and your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). The same notice is available inside the Game from the title screen and the pause menu.

## Who is responsible

The responsible party and Information Officer for the Game is **Sapphorion**, Gqeberha, South Africa.
Contact: **sapphorion@gmail.com**

## What the Game collects

The Game only collects what it needs to give you a profile and keep your park:

| Information | Required? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Director name (any name or nickname you choose) | Yes, to create a profile | Shows on your profile and end-of-run report |
| Profile picture: one of the built-in icons, **or** a photo you choose to upload (shrunk to 128 × 128 pixels) | Optional. Icons are the default | Shows on your profile |
| Game progress, saved parks, up to 3 automatic backups, career statistics and settings (sound, volume, mouse sensitivity) | Needed to save and continue | Lets you continue where you left off and recover a damaged save |
| Feedback you choose to send, and the game details you choose to include | Optional | Helps improve the game |

The Game does **not** ask for your email address, phone number, location, age, contacts or payment details. It has **no adverts, no analytics and no tracking**, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

## Where your information is kept

- **On the GitHub Pages site:** everything stays in your browser's storage on your device. It is not sent to Sapphorion or anyone else.
- **On the Claude link (claude.ai):** your profile and saves are also kept in a private area of the Game's storage on claude.ai, linked to your Claude account so you can continue on another device. That area is private to you: even Sapphorion, as the publisher, cannot read it. Anthropic provides that storage under its own privacy policy.

## Feedback and park files

If you send feedback, it goes from your own email app to Sapphorion, so your email address is visible. You choose whether to include game details (park day, rating, phase, browser and screen size). Feedback is used only to improve the Game and deleted when no longer needed. Park files you export stay wherever you save them, and are only seen by Sapphorion if you choose to send one.

## Other services the Game connects to

Loading the Game connects your browser to these services, which receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies:

- **GitHub** (hosts the GitHub Pages site)
- **Anthropic / claude.ai** (hosts the Claude link and its storage)
- **Google Fonts** (serves the Game's fonts)
- **cdnjs / Cloudflare** (serves the three.js 3D library)

These providers' servers are outside South Africa. That transfer is necessary to deliver the Game, and these providers apply their own data-protection safeguards.

## Why and on what basis

Your information is used only to run the Game features you ask for: your profile, saving and continuing your park, and your career record. The basis is your consent, which you give when you create a profile, and the need to provide the service you asked for (POPIA section 11(1)(a) and (b)). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your profile or all Game data.

## How long it is kept

Until you delete it. You can:

- edit your name and picture in **Profile**;
- delete one director with **Profile → Delete profile**;
- delete every profile, save and setting on that device, and the copies on your Claude account, with **Privacy & license → Delete all my game data**.

Clearing your browser's site data also removes the copies stored on your device.

## Children

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to read this notice and agree before you create a profile. Use one of the built-in icons instead of a photo. Never upload a photo of a child without a parent's or guardian's permission.

## Security

Saves stored on claude.ai are private to your account. Anyone who uses the same browser on the same device can see the profiles stored there, so delete your profile if you share a device.

## Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. Because the Game keeps your information on your device or in your private Claude storage, you can do most of this yourself in the Game. For anything else, email **sapphorion@gmail.com**.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the **Information Regulator (South Africa)**: [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za). Complaints are lodged through its [eServices portal](https://eservices.inforegulator.org.za/).

## Changes

If this notice changes, the effective date above will change and the Game will show the new version.
